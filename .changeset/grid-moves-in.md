The grid moves in. `@istec/grid-core` has the table hook (`useGrid`), its features and a headless hook or model for
every part of the grid; `@istec/grid-ui` renders them with Tailwind and shadcn components (`useDataGrid`, `DataGrid`).
Texts are English by default, with German in `gridMessagesDe`.
